Overview

At AKUM University, course registration still runs entirely on pen and paper. Students physically collect forms, walk across campus to get advisor signatures, and hope those paper records don't get lost. It's slow, error-prone, and simply impossible to do remotely.

Every lost form or missed signature isn't just a student's bad day. It's a registration error, a records dispute, or a semester the university has to sort out by hand.

Who actually deals with this process every semester?This section introduces the student AKUM's portal is designed for.

Meet Muna
