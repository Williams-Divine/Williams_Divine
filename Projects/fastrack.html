Fastrack

A Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to reduce product waste by connecting consumers to discounted near-expiry products from local sellers, retailers, and households through a trusted and transparent marketplace experience.

Overview

Right now, somewhere near you, a perfectly usable product is being thrown away just because its expiry date is close, while someone nearby who'd happily buy it at a discount has no easy way to find it. This happens with millions of products every day, even as many people are struggling with rising prices.

If buyers can't trust what they're seeing, or sellers can't trust who they're selling to, the marketplace doesn't just lose a sale. It loses the trust it needs to work at all.

Fastrack is a marketplace that connects those two sides safely. Buyers get real discounts on near-expiry products they can trust. Sellers recover money on stock they'd otherwise lose entirely.

So how do you design a marketplace that actually earns trust on both sides?This section covers the key decisions that shaped the product.

Key Design Decisions

    BUYERS

  • Clear expiry visibility: Expiry dates and days left are displayed clearly to reduce uncertainty.
  • Trust-first experience: Verified sellers, refund protection, and product reviews help users feel safer buying.
  • Smart savings positioning: The platform focuses on affordability and waste reduction instead of “cheap expired goods.”
  • Simple checkout flow: Pickup-based orders reduce complexity and make transactions faster.

    • SELLERS

  • Fast product listing: Sellers can upload products quickly with minimal steps and inputs.
  • Low operational stress: The experience is designed to make selling near-expiry products simple and manageable.
  • Reputation protection: Clear policies and verification systems help reduce disputes and complaints.
  • Faster product movement:Urgency indicators and discounted pricing help products sell before expiry.
