Overview

Finding a reliable handyman in Nigeria usually means asking around and hoping for the best. That word-of-mouth process comes with unpredictable pricing, no easy way to check if someone's actually skilled, and real safety concerns about letting a stranger into your home.

For a platform trying to connect people safely, that hesitation is the whole business. If customers don't trust who's showing up at their door, they simply won't book.

Who actually deals with this problem, and what do they need?This section introduces the customer HandyMan is designed for.

Meet Amina

Amina Okafor
Age 29 • Marketing Executive • Abuja

Busy Working Mother

Needs to quickly find and book trusted home service providers. The word-of-mouth process is unreliable, time-consuming, and often unsafe for herself and her family.

What decisions actually shaped this design?A few of the key choices behind the customer experience.

Key Design Decisions

  • Quick-select service pills to minimise typing
  • Ratings + structured booking flow to build trust
  • Chat-before-payment to vet workers
  • Large touch targets and icon + label pairs for accessibility

So what did designing both sides of this teach me?A short reflection before the prototype.

Learnings

Designing for two very different user groups at once taught me to balance competing needs within a single design system. Short qualitative interviews shaped which features actually made the cut.
