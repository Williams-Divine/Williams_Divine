Overview

Think about how many people quietly do the right thing for the planet — and how little of it ever gets recorded anywhere. PlanetCred was designed to solve exactly that: individuals contribute to environmental sustainability every day, but their efforts go undocumented and unverified, and organisations struggle to track real impact as a result.

The platform bridges this gap by enabling users to submit evidence of their actions and receive verified recognition for it.

👤

Define - User Context

Primary Users

  • Climate-conscious individuals (Champions)
  • Organizations managing environmental initiatives

Goals

  • Document and verify climate actions
  • Build a credible environmental portfolio

Frustrations

  • Lack of recognition for efforts
  • No trusted way to prove impact
😡

Pain Points

  • 01Difficulty proving real-world environmental impact
  • 02Lack of structured systems for tracking contributions
  • 03Low trust in self-reported climate actions
  • 04Disconnection between individuals and organizations

Ideate - Key Design Decisions

  • 📌Structured submission flow to ensure credible verification
  • ★Use of a mascot to guide and engage users emotionally
  • 💬Clear mission categories for easy participation
  • 🏆Certificate system to reward and validate contributions
  • 📱Simple, mobile-first interaction patterns
  • 🔄Seamless continuity between web and mobile experiences to ensure a consistent user journey across platforms

Learnings

If there's one thing this project taught me, it's how much trust shapes user-generated systems. Balancing simplicity with credibility required careful structuring of flows and feedback — and it reinforced how much gamification and visual guidance can do for engagement, when they're not just decoration.

This project was completed as part of my work with a company. Due to confidentiality, some details have been simplified and certain aspects of the system are not shown. All product rights and ownership belong to the organization.
