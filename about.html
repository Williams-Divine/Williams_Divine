Why hire me?

I don't start with screens. I start with the decisions people need to make.

Every high-stakes product is built to help someone make a decision.

But when workflows become confusing, information is difficult to find, or the next step isn't obvious, people hesitate, make mistakes, or rely on workarounds, and their business ends up dealing with the fallout.

That's why I look beyond individual screens.

I study how people, information, and processes interact before designing experiences that make critical tasks easier to understand and complete.

Because better interfaces don't just look cleaner.

They help people make better decisions.

I'm Divine, a Product/UX Designer who gravitates toward safety-critical and high-stakes work, helping teams design experiences that reduce errors and improve decision-making under pressure.

Designing for the people who need it most

Great safety-critical systems should work for people with different abilities, levels of technical literacy, and real-world pressures, not just ideal users.

Designs built with implementation in mind

My front-end background helps me design solutions that balance user needs with technical realities, making collaboration with engineers smoother from concept to launch.

Understanding decisions before designing screens

Every project begins by understanding what people are trying to achieve, what information they need, and where mistakes are most likely to happen.

Aligning people around better decisions

Better products come from shared understanding. I work closely with designers, developers, researchers, and stakeholders to turn complex problems into practical solutions.
