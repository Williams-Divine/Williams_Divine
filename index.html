Why hire me?

I don't start with screens. I start with the decisions people need to make.

Every healthcare product is built to help someone make a decision.

But when workflows become confusing, information is difficult to find, or the next step isn't obvious, people hesitate, make mistakes, or rely on workarounds.

That's why I look beyond individual screens.

I study how people, information, and processes interact before designing experiences that make critical tasks easier to understand and complete.

Because better interfaces don't just look cleaner.

They help people make better decisions.

I'm Divine, a Healthcare UX Designer helping product teams design safer digital experiences that reduce errors and improve decision-making in high-pressure environments.
