Finwise: Personal Finance Management

Role
Product Designer
Timeline
4 weeks
Platform
iOS & Android

Finwise helps users manage their personal finances through simplified budgeting, bill tracking, and savings workflows. The design focused on reducing financial anxiety through clarity, usability, and accessible financial education.

Product Design User Research Prototyping Usability Testing Fintech

Problem

Many people struggle with personal finance management not because they lack tools, but because existing solutions are overwhelming, full of financial jargon, or require significant financial literacy to use effectively. Users needed a system that simplified budgeting, made bill tracking intuitive, and reduced the cognitive load of financial decision-making.

Research & Discovery

Through user interviews and competitive analysis, I identified three core pain points:

  • Financial anxiety from complexity: Users felt overwhelmed by dashboards with too much data and unclear prioritization
  • Lack of actionable insights: Existing tools showed spending patterns but didn't help users understand what to do about them
  • Low financial literacy: Terms like "net worth," "liquid assets," and "debt-to-income ratio" created barriers to understanding

Design Solution

I designed Finwise around three principles:

  • Progressive disclosure: Show essential information first, allow users to drill down for details only when needed
  • Plain language: Replace financial jargon with everyday language and contextual education
  • Actionable guidance: Don't just show data—suggest what users should do next

The result was a clean, focused interface prioritizing clarity over feature density. The dashboard shows monthly budget health at a glance, with simple traffic-light indicators (green = on track, yellow = approaching limit, red = overspent).

Key Features

  • Smart Budget Tracker: Categorizes spending automatically, highlights patterns, and suggests budget adjustments based on behavior
  • Bill Reminders: Proactive notifications before due dates with contextual payment options
  • Savings Goals: Visual progress tracking with milestone celebrations to encourage continued saving
  • Financial Education: Contextual tips and explanations integrated throughout the app, not buried in a separate help section

Outcome & Learnings

Usability testing showed significant improvements in user confidence and task completion:

  • Budget setup time reduced from ~15 minutes to ~3 minutes
  • Users reported feeling "less stressed" about checking their finances
  • Financial literacy self-assessment scores improved after 2 weeks of use

What I learned: Simplicity isn't about removing features—it's about revealing them progressively based on user needs. The most impactful accessibility improvements came not from WCAG compliance (though important), but from rethinking how we use language and structure information hierarchy.

Evo-Tech Solutions: SaaS Platform Redesign

Role
Lead Product Designer
Timeline
6 weeks
Platform
Web Application

Evo-Tech Solutions needed a redesigned web platform that improved service discoverability, simplified navigation, and made their technical offerings clearer to business users without technical backgrounds.

UX Design Information Architecture UI Design SaaS B2B

Problem

Evo-Tech's existing platform had grown organically over time, resulting in inconsistent navigation, buried services, and unclear value propositions. Business users struggled to find relevant solutions, and the platform's complexity created friction in the decision-making process.

Research Insights

User interviews revealed:

  • Users couldn't distinguish between similar services or understand which one fit their needs
  • Navigation structure didn't match users' mental models of how tech solutions are organized
  • Technical jargon created barriers for decision-makers without engineering backgrounds

Solution

I restructured the information architecture around user goals rather than internal service categories. Services were reorganized into outcome-based groups ("Improve Security," "Scale Infrastructure," "Automate Workflows") with clear comparisons and use-case examples.

The new design introduced:

  • Service comparison tools to help users evaluate options
  • Contextual education explaining technical concepts in business terms
  • Streamlined navigation reducing clicks to key services by 40%
  • Clearer visual hierarchy prioritizing popular and recommended solutions

Outcome

Post-launch analytics showed:

  • Service discovery improved by 52%
  • Time-to-conversion decreased by 28%
  • Support tickets related to "how do I find X" dropped by 35%

Innovate: Simplified Task Management

Role
Product Designer
Timeline
3 weeks
Platform
iOS & Android

Innovate is a task management app designed to reduce cognitive load through smart prioritization, minimal interface design, and focus-oriented workflows.

Product Design Workflow Design Usability Mobile

Problem

Most task management apps overwhelm users with features, categories, tags, filters, and customization options. Users wanted a system that helped them focus on what actually mattered without requiring extensive setup or cognitive overhead.

Solution

Innovate uses smart prioritization algorithms to surface the most important tasks based on deadlines, user behavior, and task dependencies—without requiring users to manually assign priority levels. The interface is intentionally minimal, showing only what's actionable today.

Key Design Decisions

  • Single-view focus: No tabs, no navigation menus—just today's tasks
  • Gestural interactions for quick actions (swipe to complete, long-press to defer)
  • Contextual suggestions: "You usually complete emails in the morning—want to add one now?"

Outcome

Users reported completing 34% more tasks weekly compared to their previous systems, with significantly lower feelings of task-related stress.

QR Code Mobile: Accessible Utility App

Role
Product Designer
Timeline
2 weeks
Platform
iOS & Android

A QR code generation and scanning app designed with accessibility and usability improvements for diverse user contexts.

Mobile Design Usability Accessibility

Problem

Most QR code apps assume perfect lighting, steady hands, and clear camera focus. Users with accessibility needs—low vision, motor impairments, or older devices—struggled with existing solutions.

Solution

I designed features that improved usability for everyone:

  • Auto-stabilization with haptic feedback when QR code is detected
  • High-contrast viewfinder with adjustable overlay brightness
  • Voice feedback confirming successful scans
  • Large touch targets for code history and sharing

Outcome

Usability testing showed 87% first-scan success rate (vs. 62% industry average), with particularly strong improvements for users with motor impairments and in low-light conditions.
